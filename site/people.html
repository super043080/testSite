Bernard Wu, Ph.D.

I earned my Sc.B. and A.B. at Brown University, then my M.A. and Ph.D. at Princeton University, where my research used optical steganography to strengthen the physical layer security of fiber-optic networks. I then worked at several investment banks in equity derivatives, most recently for about 10.5 years as a trader at UBS in Hong Kong.
