Knowing the name of a concept is not the same as understanding it.

When a subject is explained in a convoluted way, or too much arrives at once, students struggle and lose confidence. So I start by finding out what your child already knows, and refine that understanding before building on it.

Then I introduce topics before school reaches them. When the teacher covers the material later, the lesson becomes reinforcement, which matters because students spend most of their time in school.

The aim is for students to reason from first principles, develop their own way of solving problems, and progress with confidence and independence.
